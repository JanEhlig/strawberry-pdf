Export to Word and Excel

- New in the ⋯ menu: Export to Word and Export to Excel.
- Word files keep paragraphs, fonts, sizes, colors, letter spacing, tables and images, with one section per page in the original page size.
- Excel files get one sheet per page. Tables keep their rows and columns, and amounts, percentages and dates become real numbers you can calculate with.
- Filled form fields, added text and other annotations are part of the export.
- Scanned pages with recognized text are exported as editable text.
- After exporting, the file can be opened right away.
