Print dialog

- Ctrl+P now opens a print dialog with a preview instead of the plain Windows dialog.
- Choose the printer, number of copies, pages (all, the current page or a range such as 1-3, 5), paper size, scaling, orientation and two-sided printing.
- Landscape pages are turned automatically to fit the paper.
- Print in grayscale and choose whether annotations are printed. Filled form fields are always printed.
- The printer and settings are remembered for next time.
- The Windows print dialog is still available for special printer settings.
